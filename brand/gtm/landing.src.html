How it works

One model. Every drawing.

Most tools make you redraw the same thing four times and then hope the four agree. Storystick keeps one model and derives everything else from it — so a change in one place is a change everywhere.

{{AUDIENCE}}

A story stick is the oldest planning tool there is. Mark it once, and the measurement stops being something you can get wrong.

Carpenters have used a marked stick instead of a tape for centuries — not because it's simpler, but because it's more reliable. Read a tape forty times and you get forty chances to be off by a sixteenth. Mark a stick once and you get none.

That's the whole idea behind the software. Capture a dimension once. Let it carry.
